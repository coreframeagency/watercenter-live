About Water Center

Water Center supplies and fits pumps, filters, purifiers and hot water systems for homes, farms and businesses across Sri Lanka.

We work from two branches, Colombo and Kurunegala. The same team surveys the site, supplies the unit, installs it and services it afterwards, so you deal with one company from the first call.

We also advise on any water problem, even when the answer is not a purchase.

What we supply and fit

Six lines of work. Every price is for the unit fitted, with the warranty written on the quote.

Any water problem? Talk to us first.

Cloudy or smelly water, scale from hard water, low pressure, a borewell that runs dry, a tank that never fills. We give consultation on any water problem, find the cause, and tell you what to do, even when the answer is not a purchase.

  • Cloudy, smelly or salty water
